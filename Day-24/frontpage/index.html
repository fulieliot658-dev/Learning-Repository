Frontpage collects the blogs, newsletters and changelogs you follow into a single reading dashboard. No algorithm, no infinite scroll — just your sources, your categories, and a digest that tells you what you missed.

A digest that respects your time

Check in once a day and get a ranked catch-up: one lead story, the best of each source, and everything else collapsed out of the way.

Three ways to read

Dense list when you're triaging, comfortable list when you're browsing, magazine cards when you want the pictures. Switch any time.

Built for the keyboard

j and k to move, o to open, s to save, / to search, ⌘K for anything else. Your hands never have to leave the home row.

Feeds you don't have to hunt for

Start from a curated pack, browse the catalogue by topic, paste a URL, or import an OPML file from whatever reader you're leaving.
